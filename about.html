About

Information security analyst who turns technical know-how into practical protection. I help organizations find weaknesses before attackers do, keep their people and systems running smoothly, and build secure websites.

Hello there

Hi, I’m Deborah, a security analyst who finds out what happened, how it happened, and how to stop it from happening again

I’m a cybersecurity analyst in Nairobi who loves figuring out how things break, and then making sure they don’t break again. I got into security through curiosity: I wanted to understand how attacks work so I could stop them. That curiosity still drives everything I do, from monitoring for threats in a SOC to tracing an incident back to its source.

I also build. I design and develop websites with security in mind from the first line of code, because protection works better when it’s part of the foundation. On this site you’ll find my security labs, investigations and web projects, along with notes on what I learned from each one.
